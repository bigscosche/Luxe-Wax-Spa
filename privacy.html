Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Luxe Wax Spa ("we," "our," "us") operates the website located at luxewaxspa.beauty. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and secure your information when you visit our website or submit inquiries through our online forms.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide to us. This happens primarily when you submit our lead capture or quote request forms:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Service Preferences: The type of service you are interested in (e.g., Brazilian Wax, Brow Design, Lash Lift) and your closest location.
  • Inquiry Details: Any specific message or questions you write in the optional comments field.
  • Redirection Data: We do not process credit cards or take calendar reservations directly on our website. All formal bookings are processed externally by our scheduling vendor, Vagaro.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to run our spa operations and answer client inquiries:

  • To respond directly to your questions, quotes, and booking requests.
  • To send you appointment notifications, reminders, or updates (if you explicitly consent or book through our partners).
  • To analyze website usage and ensure our pages render properly.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business under strict confidentiality terms:

  • Supabase: We store secure form submissions in our private database hosted by Supabase.
  • Slack: We receive real-time internal booking notifications on our administrative channels.
  • Vagaro: Our calendar booking partner. Their privacy rules apply when you leave our site to book an appointment.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement a variety of security measures to keep your personal information safe. Our database connections utilize encrypted secure sockets (SSL), and administrative bypasses have been disabled. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights & Access

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold. If you wish to make a request, please contact us at @luxewaxspact or visit our suite space.
